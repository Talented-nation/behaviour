Privacy Policy

Phone Book by TeaRex

Last Updated: January 2026

Summary: Phone Book does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your contacts and information stay on your device.

1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing Phone Book ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information.

Phone Book is designed with privacy as the top priority. The App:

  • Does not collect personal data
  • Does not collect device information
  • Does not collect usage data or analytics
  • Does not access your phone's built-in contacts
  • Does not require any special permissions
  • Does not transmit any data over the internet

3. How Your Data is Stored

All contacts and information you enter into Phone Book are stored locally on your device only. The App uses a local database (SQLite) that exists solely on your phone or tablet.

This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • Your data is not backed up to any cloud service
  • Your data is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Your data is not shared with any other applications

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your information depends on your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Using a secure lock screen (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Being cautious about which apps you install on your device

5. Third-Party Services

Phone Book does not integrate with any third-party services. There are:

  • No analytics tools
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations
  • No cloud storage connections

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, there is no risk to children's privacy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual contacts within the App
  • Clear the App's data in your device settings
  • Uninstall the App entirely

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any of your data, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete. You have complete control over all information entered into the App, as it exists only on your device.
